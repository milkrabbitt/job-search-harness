# Know why you're applying.

Job Search Harness is an open-source job-search workspace that runs on your computer, bringing together resume evidence, job requirements, salary comparisons, and follow-up records.

## Short launch post

Which part of your experience supports this application? What is missing from the job description? What did the recruiter say to do next?

**Job Search Harness v0.3.0** brings those questions into one workspace:

- **Match with evidence.** Upload a PDF, review and confirm the extracted text, then optionally authorize your chosen model to compare it with the JD. Review relevant experience, gaps, and unknowns with references to the supplied text.
- **Compare the actual terms.** Put 2–6 saved roles side by side. See original salary quotes, currencies, pay periods, and explicit payment counts. Annualize CNY monthly pay only when payment counts are explicit; keep missing information visible.
- **Keep follow-ups organized.** A verified, authorized 126 mailbox can handle supported routine messages and one follow-up. Interviews and employment terms go back to you for a decision. The 126 credential integration currently requires macOS Keychain; BOSS and Liepin chats remain manual browser workflows.

The current interface is **Chinese**. Bring a supported model service or start with local rules. The offline demo uses fictional data and simulated messages, so you can explore without your real resume or mailbox.

Explore the project: <https://github.com/milkrabbitt/job-search-harness>

If this belongs in your next job search, **Star it to find it again**. Feedback from trying the demo is welcome.
